# Sandyfeet Liwliwa Camp — Chatbot Knowledge Base

## About Sandyfeet
Sandyfeet is a beachfront resort and camp located in Liwliwa, San Felipe, Zambales, Philippines. It offers a relaxing coastal escape with rooms, day tours, camping, and various outdoor activities. The resort is known for its stunning waterfront views, pool area, bonfire setups, and laid-back beach vibe.

## Location
- **Address**: Liwliwa, San Felipe, Zambales, Philippines
- **Nearby landmarks**: Liwliwa Beach, Crystal Beach
- **Distance from Manila**: Approximately 4–5 hours by car or bus

## How to Get Here
- **By Bus**: Take a Victory Liner bus from Cubao, Pasay, or Caloocan going to Iba, Zambales. Ask the conductor to drop you off at San Felipe town proper. From the highway in San Felipe, take a tricycle to Liwliwa (about 15–20 minutes).
- **By Private Car**: From Manila, take NLEX → SCTEX → Exit at Subic. Turn left at the Olongapo Public Cemetery and follow the road going to San Felipe, then continue to Liwliwa.
- **Estimated Bus Fare**: Around ₱350–₱500 one-way (depending on bus type and terminal)
- **Tricycle from San Felipe to Liwliwa**: Around ₱100–₱150

## Room Types and Capacity

### Group / Barkada Room
- **Maximum capacity**: 14 guests
- **Best for**: Friend groups (barkada), celebrations, group getaways
- **Extra guest charge**: ₱200 per additional person per night (for guests beyond the included capacity per room unit)
- **Features**: Spacious layout, great for parties and group activities

### Ground Floor Room
- **Best for**: Small groups or families
- **Features**: Air-conditioned, located on the ground floor for easy access, comes with basic amenities
- **Extra guest charge**: Additional charge applies per extra person per night when exceeding the room's base capacity (check the Rooms page for current rates)

### Couple Room
- **Best for**: Couples or solo travelers
- **Features**: Intimate and cozy, comfortable and private
- **Extra guest charge**: Additional charge applies per extra person per night when exceeding the room's base capacity (check the Rooms page for current rates)

### Tent / Camping
- **Best for**: Adventurous guests who enjoy outdoor camping
- **Features**: Located within the resort grounds, near the beach area

### How Extra Guest Charges Work
- Each room type has a base capacity (minimum guests included in the room price)
- If your group exceeds the base capacity, an additional charge per person per night applies
- Example: If a room's base capacity is 8 and you have 10 guests, you pay extra for 2 guests × the additional charge × number of nights
- Visit the **Rooms page** for exact pricing and capacity per room type

## Room Pricing
- Room prices are per room per night
- Total cost = (room price × number of rooms × number of nights) + extra guest charges
- Down payment is 50% of the total cost
- Balance is due upon check-in
- Visit the **Rooms page** on the website for the most up-to-date pricing

## Booking Process
1. **Browse rooms or day tours** on the website
2. **Select your preferred dates** using the calendar (must book at least 2 days in advance)
3. **Choose room type and quantity** — set the number of adults and kids per room
4. **Fill in guest information** (name, email, contact number)
5. **Submit your reservation** — you will receive a booking reference number
6. **Upload payment proof** (GCash or bank transfer)
7. **Track your reservation** using your reference number and email on the "Track Reservation" page

## Check-in and Check-out Times
- **Check-in**: 2:00 PM (14:00)
- **Check-out**: 12:00 PM (noon)
- Check-in and check-out times can be adjusted during booking within allowed windows
- **No early check-in or late check-out fees** — times are fixed per booking selection

## Day Tour

- **Maximum capacity**: 38 guests per day

- Day tour includes access to the pool, facilities, and resort grounds
